Micro-Sales Strategy

  • Price: R19–R59 (impulse range). Never require account for one-time tools.
  • Distribution: Gumroad, Chrome Web Store, Play Store, Figma Community, Product Hunt.
  • Build: 2–4 days each with OpenCode. Ship weekly cadence.
  • Revenue math: 200 sales × R39 = R7,800 per app. 8 apps = R62k. Plus upsells.
  • Marketing: Short TikTok/Reels demos; SEO "free [tool]"; Reddit r/SideProject.
